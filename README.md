This is a browser-based notetaking and journaling app designed to be completely self-contained on one device. 
Amelda is available at https://www.amelda.net/

Amelda is a cloudy-free notes and journaling application.

* You own your data.
* Your data is saved on your device.
* Works offline.
* You decide when to share it and with whom.
* We will never collect your data or give it to a third party.

Amelda is semi-structured. This means that individual notes can be connected to organize and navigate between notes. See the instructions at amelda.net for more info.
